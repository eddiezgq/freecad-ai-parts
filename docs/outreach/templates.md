# 厂商资料索取邮件模板（ADR-0050）

用 `{厂商}`、`{产品线}`、`{型号范围}` 替换。按对方语言选中文或英文；每封按厂商实际产品改写第二段，不群发同一封信。

## 中文

**主题：** 问渠 WenQuest：申请收录{厂商}{产品线}资料

{厂商}市场部 / 销售部负责同志：

您好！我们是问渠（WenQuest，wenquestrobotics.com），正在建设一个 AI 原生的工程选型平台：工程师说出性能要求，系统从各厂商的标准件中选型、校核转矩、转速和安装接口，生成三维布局和带厂商型号的物料清单。平台同时为高校提供免费的教学资源。

贵公司的{产品线}（{型号范围}）是我们希望收录的产品。为此想向您申请：

1. 公开的产品样本或规格书（含外形尺寸图）；如有三维模型（STEP），也请告知获取方式；
2. 一句书面同意：允许我们把产品参数与安装接口尺寸收入选型平台，包括平台将来的收费版本；以及是否允许在平台上提供贵公司 CAD 的下载链接。

几点说明：

- 收录免费；选型排序只依据校核结果和公开规则，不受厂商影响，选型理由对用户公开；
- 所有参数都注明出处页码，贵公司可以随时要求更正或撤下；
- 我们是在建设中的平台，目前没有具体的采购需求，请不必按询价处理。

如有不便，回复“不需要”即可，我们不再打扰。谢谢！

问渠 WenQuest
张国庆
parts@wenquestrobotics.com
https://wenquestrobotics.com

## English

**Subject:** WenQuest: request to include {vendor} {product line} in our selection platform

Dear {vendor} team,

We are WenQuest (wenquestrobotics.com), building an AI-native engineering selection platform: an engineer states the performance requirements, and the system selects standard parts from vendor catalogs, checks torque, speed and mounting interfaces, and produces a 3D layout and a bill of materials with vendor part numbers. The platform also provides free teaching resources to universities.

We would like to include your {product line} ({model range}). Could you help with:

1. Your public catalog or datasheets, including dimension drawings, and how to obtain 3D models (STEP) if available;
2. Written permission to include product parameters and mounting dimensions in the platform, including future paid versions, and whether we may link to your CAD downloads.

Please note:

- Inclusion is free. Ranking depends only on engineering checks and published rules, never on vendors, and the reasons are shown to users;
- Every value cites its source page, and you may ask us to correct or remove anything at any time;
- The platform is under construction and we have no immediate purchase requirement, so this is not a request for quotation.

If you prefer not to take part, a one-line reply is enough and we will not contact you again. Thank you!

WenQuest
Guoqing (Eddie) Zhang
parts@wenquestrobotics.com
https://wenquestrobotics.com
