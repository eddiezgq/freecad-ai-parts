# This plant: WenQuest Digital Industrial Park · Computer Plant

> The eighth plant in the park: **desktops, 14-inch notebooks, 2U dual-socket servers, 42U racks and industrial PCs**.
> Mainboards, memory modules, chassis and notebook housings are made here; CPUs, DRAM, SSDs, displays, batteries and power supplies are bought in — CPUs and DRAM need a wafer fab, which the park's chip packaging plant is not.

## The park chain

```
Computer plant (industrial PC WQ-IPC) → Robot plant (inside the robot controller cabinet)
```

The robot plant orders item IPC here (our item WQ-IPC; split out of the controller cabinet from round 166). Racks WQ-RACK42 go to computing centers and data centers (example customers).

## Shop floor

Five lines, 29 machines (layout, sizes and rates are example values):

- **Boards and memory (SMT)**: incoming inspection, paste printing, SPI, two placers, reflow, AOI, **X-ray** (BGA voids), selective soldering, ICT, BIOS programming, **memory tester** (8 sockets);
- **Structural parts**: laser cutting, press brake, self-clinching press, powder coating; two machining centers and an anodizing line (notebook housings);
- **Systems**: two thermal-paste dispensers (dp-1, dp-2), desktop / notebook / server assembly stations, POST & OS imaging, **burn-in room**, system test;
- **Rack integration bench**, packing, stores and two AGVs.
