# 实验 7　数字工厂闭环 · Lab 7 Closing the Loop in the Digital Factory

**学时 Time**：2 小时 / 2 hours　**分组 Groups**：1–5 人一组，每人轮流扮演各岗位 / 1–5 per group, rotating roles
**平台 Platform**：问渠数字工厂工作台（教学模式）/ WenQuest Digital Factory workbench (teaching mode)

## 一、实验目的 · Objectives

1. 读懂一座工厂的运营总览看板，能从指标中找出问题并判断原因。
   Read a factory's operations dashboard, spot problems in the indicators and reason about their causes.
2. 走通“接单 → 算料 → 设计 → 加工 → 检验 → 入库 → 成本”的完整闭环，理解每一步的数据从哪里来、到哪里去。
   Walk the complete loop — order, MRP, design, machining, inspection, stock, cost — and see where each step's data comes from and goes.
3. 理解统一数据总线：不同软件（ERPNext、FreeCAD、车间终端、AI）如何通过同一条总线协同工作。
   Understand the unified data bus: how different tools (ERPNext, FreeCAD, the shop-floor terminal, the AI) work together through one bus.

## 二、背景 · Background

问渠减速器厂只生产一种产品：二级圆柱齿轮减速器 WQR-105（传动比 10.5，售价 $1650）。本实验聚焦其中的输出轴 SH-301，工艺路线为：
WenQuest Gearbox makes one product, the WQR-105 two-stage gear reducer (ratio 10.5, $1650). This lab follows its output shaft SH-301 through the routing:

| 工序 Operation | 分钟/件 min/part | 设备 Machine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 下料 Sawing | 3 | SAW-01 带锯床 |
| 粗车 Rough turning | 18 | CNC-L01-A 数控车床 |
| 调质 Quench & temper | 12 | HT-01 热处理炉（一炉 20 件 / 20 parts per load） |
| 精车 Finish turning | 15 | CNC-L01-B 数控车床 |
| 铣键槽 Keyway milling | 10 | KEY-01 键槽铣床 |
| 磨外圆 Cylindrical grinding | 12 | GRD-01 外圆磨床 |
| 零件检验 Part inspection | 6 | QC-01 检验站（三坐标 / CMM） |

实验开始时，工厂已经运行了几天：本月已交付 12 单，今天的输出轴工单正在加工，磨床刚刚停机换砂轮。
When the lab starts the factory has been running for a few days: 12 orders delivered this month, today's shaft work order in progress, and the grinder has just stopped to change its wheel.

## 三、准备（教师）· Preparation (instructor)

1. 按 `digital/README.md` 启动平台，确认 `http://localhost:8100` 能打开、左下角显示“统一数据总线 已连接”。
   Start the platform as in `digital/README.md`; check that the workbench opens and the bus shows “connected”.
2. 以“厂长”角色、教学模式登录，进入“经营与成本”，在教师控制台点“重置实验 7 情景”（勾选“同时清空学生得分”）。
   Log in as Plant manager in teaching mode, open “经营与成本” and reset the Lab 7 scenario (tick “clear scores”).
3. 告诉学生本组订单的交期（默认 10 月 15 日）。任务 4 开始前，把仿真倍速调到 20×（约 15 分钟做完 10 根轴）。
   Tell students their order's due date (default 15 October). Before task 4, set the simulation speed to 20× (about 15 minutes for 10 shafts).

## 四、实验步骤 · Procedure

每人用自己的名字登录（系统按名字记分）。顶栏“角色”可以随时切换；每个任务注明了建议角色。
Everyone logs in with their own name (scores are kept per name). Switch roles in the top bar at any time; each task names a suggested role.

### 任务 1　读懂首页看板（厂长，15 分）· Task 1 Read the dashboard (Plant manager, 15 pts)

打开首页，回答三个问题（在“实验 7 · 任务”页提交）：
Open the home page and answer (submit on the “实验 7 · 任务” page):
- 现在哪台设备停机？Which machine is down now?
- 哪张在手订单有延期风险？Which open order is at risk of being late?
- 哪种物料低于安全库存？Which material is below safety stock?

思考 Think：磨床停机为什么会影响订单？看“车间实况”里磨床前排队了几件、“订单与交期”里哪张订单在等这些零件。
Why does a grinder stoppage affect an order? Look at the queue in front of the grinder and which order is waiting for those parts.

### 任务 2　接单与算料（计划员，20 分）· Task 2 Take the order and run MRP (Planner, 20 pts)

绿谷输送设备订购 10 台 WQR-105。在“订单与计划”新建订单 → 点“算料” → 读懂算料表 → 提交 → 在预览里确认。确认后桥接把销售订单、SH-301 工单和请购单写入 ERPNext，可以到 ERPNext 里核对。
GreenValley Conveyor orders 10 × WQR-105. In “订单与计划”, create the order → “算料” (MRP) → read the table → submit → confirm in the preview. The bridge then writes the sales order, the SH-301 work order and the material request into ERPNext; check them there.

### 任务 3　设计发布（工艺员，15 分）· Task 3 Release a design change (Process engineer, 15 pts)

客户要求把输出轴键槽长度由 45 mm 改为 42 mm。在“设计与工艺”页的“在线设计”里把槽长 L 改成 42，看三维模型和校核结果，填改动说明，点“发布新版本”。查看新版本、零件图和键槽 G 代码；读 AI 的提醒——已经在加工的零件怎么办？
The customer wants the keyway shortened from 45 mm to 42 mm. In “设计与工艺” → “在线设计”, set the keyway length L to 42, check the 3D model and the design check, add a change note and press “发布新版本” (publish). Check the new revision, the drawing and the keyway G-code; read the AI's alert — what happens to parts already in progress?

进阶（选做）Advanced (optional)：装 FreeCAD 1.1，在同一页下载“我的 FreeCAD 宏包”，改 `wq_shaft.py` 的 `PARAMS` 后执行 `wq_publish.py`，效果相同。Install FreeCAD 1.1, download your macro pack on the same page, edit `PARAMS` in `wq_shaft.py` and run `wq_publish.py` — same result.

### 任务 4　车间执行（操作工，20 分）· Task 4 Run the shop floor (Operator, 20 pts)

在“订单与计划”点你的工单的“下达车间”。到“车间执行”逐台设备点“开工”——每道工序都要有人开工，零件才会往下走。打开“3D 车间”观察 AGV 送料和排队。直到 10 根轴全部完工。
Release your work order to the shop in “订单与计划”. In “车间执行”, press “开工” (start) on each machine — every operation needs someone to start it before parts move on. Watch AGVs and queues in the 3D shop. Continue until all 10 shafts are finished.

思考 Think：哪道工序是瓶颈？为什么粗车前没有排队，磨床前却排了队？
Which operation is the bottleneck? Why is there a queue in front of the grinder but not in front of rough turning?

### 任务 5　质检与处置（质检员，15 分）· Task 5 Inspect and decide (Quality inspector, 15 pts)

在“质量”页看轴承位 Ø35 k6 的控制图和测量表。如果有超差零件，对不合格品做出处置（返修、报废或让步接收），并回答：轴承位直径为什么会逐渐变大？
In “质量”, study the control chart for the Ø35 k6 bearing seat. Disposition any non-conforming parts (rework, scrap or use-as-is) and answer: why does the bearing-seat diameter creep upward?

### 任务 6　成本分析（厂长，15 分）· Task 6 Analyse the cost (Plant manager, 15 pts)

在“经营与成本”选你的工单，读成本分解。提交：实际加工成本比标准高百分之几？主要原因是什么？再问 AI 工厂助手同样的问题，比较它的提示和你的结论。
In “经营与成本”, choose your work order and read the cost breakdown. Submit: by what percentage does the actual machining cost exceed standard, and why? Then ask the AI factory assistant and compare.

### 选做　用推演选处理方案（厂长或计划员，不计分）· Optional: choose a fix by simulation

老师先在教师控制台把车间切到“按班次”，再让一台设备停机（例如 CNC-L01-B 停 4 小时）。

1. 打开“厂情中枢 → 异常处理”，找到这条停机异常。右栏会自动推演几个方案：不处理、恢复后加班、改派、维修加急。
2. 比较各方案：订单 SAL-ORD-2026-00019 的预计交期、多花的钱。系统推荐的是哪个？你同意吗？为什么？
3. 去“推演工作台”试一个系统没给的组合（例如加班 1 小时 + 维修加急），保存成场景，和推荐方案并排比较。
4. 把你选的方案提交为动作，请老师（或扮演厂长的同学）批准，然后在车间看它是否真的生效。

The instructor switches the shop to shift mode and stops a machine. In “厂情中枢 → 异常处理” compare the simulated options (do nothing, overtime, reassign, expedite repair) by the due date of SAL-ORD-2026-00019 and the extra cost; try your own combination in the simulation workbench, submit the chosen fix as an action and see it executed after approval.

## 五、实验报告 · Report

1. 画出本实验中一条订单数据的流向：从你在工作台提交，到出现在 ERPNext、车间设备和首页看板上，经过了哪些主题（topic）？
   Draw the path of your order's data: from the workbench, through which bus topics, into ERPNext, the machines and the dashboard.
2. 用任务 2 的数字解释 MRP 的净需求公式：净需求 = 毛需求 + 其他订单占用 + 安全库存 − 库存 − 在途。
   Explain the net-requirement formula with your task-2 numbers.
3. 用任务 4 的观察说明瓶颈对交期的影响，提出一条改进措施并估算效果。
   Use your task-4 observations to explain how the bottleneck drives delivery; propose one improvement and estimate its effect.
4. 用控制图说明“尚未超差”和“需要处理”的区别，质检员应在什么时候介入？
   Using the control chart, distinguish “still in tolerance” from “needs action”. When should the inspector step in?
5. 标准成本与实际成本为什么不同？在真实工厂里怎样缩小差距？
   Why do standard and actual cost differ, and how would a real factory narrow the gap?
