数据：零件模型、程序和工艺卡只在你的电脑上。使用 AI 起草工艺（专业版）时，只把零件特征（直径、长度、槽、螺纹、孔）、材料组和候选刀具的
样本范围发给你选择的 AI 服务（DeepSeek 或 Claude），不发模型文件。
Data: part models, programs and process cards stay on your computer. When AI process drafting (Pro) is used, only the part features
(diameters, lengths, grooves, threads, holes), the material group and the catalogue ranges of the candidate tools are sent to the AI
service you choose (DeepSeek or Claude), never the model file.

安全：程序只在整台机床仿真通过后输出，但仿真用的是你填的机床、刀具和夹具数据；首件必须空运行、单段试切确认。
Safety: programs are output only after the whole-machine simulation passes, but the simulation uses the machine, tool and fixture
data you entered; always dry-run the first part and confirm it in single block.
